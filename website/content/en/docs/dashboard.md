# Web Dashboard

The **Web Dashboard** at [dikta.me/en/dashboard](https://dikta.me/en/dashboard) is where you manage your account outside the Windows app — buy credits, check your license, update your profile, and control newsletter preferences. Any browser, any device, any OS.

![Dashboard Overview](/images/docs/dashboard-overview.png)

> [!TIP]
> The Windows app's [Account settings](settings/account.md) shows the same license tier and credit balance locally. The dashboard is the **active** side of that relationship — it's where you actually change things.

## Overview

Sign in with the same account you linked in the app's setup wizard. The landing page shows three stat cards and a row of Quick Actions:

- **Credits** — your current wallet balance with a link to the full transaction history.
- **License** — your tier (Free, Power) and whether it's perpetual or time-limited.
- **Activity** — total transactions and the date of your last session.

**Quick Actions** shortcut to the waitlist page (Get the App), these docs (Documentation), and your Profile (Settings).

## Wallet

![Dashboard — Wallet panel](/images/docs/dashboard-wallet.png)

The Wallet panel shows your **current credit balance** and a full chronological history of every credit movement. Each row tags the type:

- **Usage** (red) — credits consumed by dictation, vision, or TTS pipelines.
- **Purchase** (green) — credits added by a top-up.
- **Promo** / **Adjustment** — credits granted manually or by promo code.

Rows also show how many requests were served that day. Click into a day to see the per-request breakdown.

## Profile

![Dashboard — Profile settings](/images/docs/dashboard-profile.png)

The Profile panel manages your identity:

- **Avatar** — click the circle to upload and crop a new photo. Syncs back to the Windows app on next launch.
- **Email** — read-only; tied to your account's sign-in provider. Contact support if you need to change it.
- **Name** — display name used in the app and in support tickets.
- **Account Created** — when you first signed up.

## Newsletter

![Dashboard — Newsletter preferences](/images/docs/dashboard-newsletter.png)

The Newsletter panel is a **one-click opt-in/opt-out** for each language-specific newsletter. Because your email is already verified by your account, every change takes effect instantly — no confirmation email needed.

- **English newsletter** — Weekly literary news, posted in English.
- **Spanish newsletter** — Noticias literarias semanales, en español.

You can be subscribed to both, one, or neither.

## Signing out

The **Sign Out** button on the Overview page ends your browser session. It does **not** sign you out of the Windows app — those are independent sessions.
