# Voice Macros

**Voice Macros** are your secret weapon for speed. They turn short phrases into long blocks of text or complex commands.

## What is a Macro?

It's a mapping: **Trigger Phrase** → **Big Content**.

When you're dictating, dIKta.me listens for these triggers. If it hears one, it swaps it out instantly.

## Cool Uses

### 1. Signatures & Templates
Stop typing the same email closer 50 times a day.
- **You say**: "Insert Signature"
- **We type**:
  ```text
  Best regards,

  **John Doe**
  Senior Developer | Architecture Team
  *Sent via dIKta.me*
  ```

### 2. AI Instructions
Tell the AI *how* to write, not just *what* to write.
- **You say**: "Fix Grammar"
- **We send**: "Rewrite the last sentence to be more professional and fix any grammatical errors."

### 3. Developer Shortcuts
- **You say**: "Git Push"
- **We type**: `git push origin main --force-with-lease`

## How do I add my own?

Head to **Settings > Macros**.
Keep your triggers unique (e.g., use "My Email" instead of just "Email") so you don't accidentally trigger them in conversation.
