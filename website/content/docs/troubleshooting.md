# Troubleshooting

Sometimes things break. Let's fix them.

## 🎤 "I can't hear you"

**Symptom**: You press the hotkey, but nothing happens. Or the tray icon stays gray.
- **Check 1**: Is your microphone plugged in? (Sounds silly, but it happens).
- **Check 2**: Windows Permissions. Go to **Settings > Privacy > Microphone** and make sure apps are allowed to listen.
- **Check 3**: Did you pick the right mic in dIKta.me settings?

## ⌨️ "My hotkeys aren't working"

**Symptom**: You press `Ctrl+Alt+D` and Discord opens instead (or nothing happens).
- **Fix**: Another app stole the hotkey. Right-click dIKta.me > **Settings > Hotkeys** and change it to something unique.

## ☁️ "API Key Invalid"

**Symptom**: It says "Auth Error".
- **Fix**: You probably missed a character when copying. Make sure you got the whole key (usually starts with `sk-` or `AIza-`). Watch out for accidental spaces at the start or end.

## 🐢 "It's too slow"

- **Fix**: Cloud modes depend on your internet. If your WiFi is spotty, try **Local Whisper** mode (if your computer is powerful enough). It's consistently fast, offline.
