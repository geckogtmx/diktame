# Dictation Mode

**Dictation** is the heart of dIKta.me. It listens to you and types what it hears. Simple as that.

## How it Works

1.  **Hotkey**: Press **`Ctrl+Alt+D`**.
2.  **Speak**: Just talk naturally.
3.  **Release**: Let go of the keys, and your words appear on screen.

> **Tip**: You can also use "Toggle Mode" (press once to start, press again to stop) if you're giving a long speech.

## Why it's Better

Unlike old-school dictation, dIKta.me isn't just listening for words—it's listening for **meaning**.
- It cleans up "um's" and "uh's".
- It handles punctuation for you (mostly).
- It formats things nicely (lists, dates, etc.).

## Pro Tips

- **Speak Like a Human**: Don't robot-talk. The AI understands context.
- **Microphone Matters**: A good headset makes a huge difference compared to a laptop mic.
- **Punctuation**: You *can* say "comma" or "new line" if you want full control, but usually you don't have to.
