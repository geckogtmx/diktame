# Note Mode

**Note Mode** is for your "shower thoughts." Don't lose focus on your current work—just capture the idea and keep moving.

## How it Works

1.  **Hotkey**: Press **`Ctrl+Alt+N`**.
2.  **Speak**: "Remember to buy milk" or "Fix bug on line 52."
3.  **Release**:
    - You'll hear a subtle *saved* sound.
    - **Nothing happens on screen.** You stay in your flow.
    - Ideally, you forget you even made the note until later.

## Where do they go?

By default, they live in a daily file:
`%APPDATA%\DiktaMe\notes\YYYY-MM-DD.md`

But you can pipe them anywhere:
- **Obsidian**: Send them straight to your daily note.
- **Logseq**: Append to your journal.
- **Desktop**: Just a simple text file on your desktop.
