# Ask Mode

**Ask Mode** turns your microphone into a direct line to a Genius. Stop Googling, start asking.

## How it Works

1.  **Hotkey**: Press **`Ctrl+Alt+A`**.
2.  **Ask**: "How do I center a div in CSS?" or "What's the capital of Mongolia?"
3.  **Release**:
    - The answer is **copied to your clipboard**.
    - OR typed out right where your cursor is (if you prefer).

## Use Cases

- **Coding**: "Write a regex for email validation."
- **Writing**: "给我5个关于咖啡的博客标题创意" (Give me 5 blog post ideas about coffee).
- **Facts**: "Convert 50 miles to kilometers."

## Pro Tip

Ask Mode typically uses your smartest model (like Gemini Pro or GPT-4o) because you want the *best* answer, not just the fastest one.
