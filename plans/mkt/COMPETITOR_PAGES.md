## Page 1: dIKta.me vs Wispr Flow

**URL (future):** `/vs/wispr-flow`
**Meta title:** dIKta.me vs Wispr Flow — Which Voice Dictation Tool Is Right for You?
**Meta description:** Wispr Flow is cloud-only at $12/mo. dIKta.me runs locally, costs $20 once, and lets you choose your AI model. Here's how they compare.

### TL;DR

Wispr Flow is polished, cross-platform, and backed by $700M in valuation. dIKta.me is local-first, model-agnostic, and $20 one-time. If you want a managed cloud experience with enterprise compliance, Wispr wins. If you want privacy, model freedom, and no subscription, dIKta.me wins.

### Comparison Table

| | Wispr Flow | dIKta.me |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $12/mo ($144/year) | $20 once (early bird) |
| **Platform** | Mac, Windows, iOS, Android | Windows (Mac planned V3) |
| **Privacy** | Cloud-only, SOC 2 compliant | Local-first, air-gapped option |
| **Offline mode** | No | Yes (Whisper + Ollama + Kokoro) |
| **Choose your AI model** | No (proprietary) | Yes (4 STT, 5 LLM, 5 TTS providers) |
| **Vision/Screenshot AI** | No | Yes (OCR, table extraction, describe) |
| **Voice editing (Command Mode)** | Yes | Not yet (planned V2.1) |
| **Enterprise compliance** | SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA | MIT open source (self-audit) |
| **Text injection** | At cursor, any app | At cursor, any app |
| **AI post-processing** | Fixed pipeline | Configurable (16 custom prompt slots) |
| **TTS read-back** | No | Yes (5 providers) |
| **Open source** | No | Yes (MIT) |

### Where Wispr Flow wins

- **Cross-platform.** Mac, Windows, iOS, Android. dIKta.me is Windows-only today.
- **Command Mode.** Voice-driven editing ("make that bold", "delete last paragraph"). dIKta.me has Refine mode but not free-form voice commands yet.
- **Enterprise compliance.** SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA. If your company requires these certifications, Wispr has them.
- **Polish.** $700M in funding buys a lot of design and QA. Wispr has a larger team and smoother onboarding.

### Where dIKta.me wins

- **Privacy.** Everything can run locally. Whisper for STT, Ollama for LLM, Kokoro for TTS. No data leaves your machine. Wispr sends everything to their cloud.
- **Model freedom.** Use Deepgram, Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, or any Ollama model. Wispr locks you into their proprietary pipeline.
- **Price.** $20 once vs $144/year. After 2 months, dIKta.me has paid for itself.
- **Vision AI.** Screenshot anything, get AI analysis. OCR, table extraction, visual Q&A. Wispr doesn't do this.
- **Convergence.** dIKta.me replaces your dictation tool + grammar checker + AI chat + screenshot tool. Wispr does dictation only.
- **Open source.** Audit the code. Fork it. Extend it. Wispr is a black box.

### Who should use Wispr Flow

- Teams that need enterprise compliance certifications
- Mac and mobile users who need cross-platform
- Users who want a fully managed experience with zero setup
- People who value Command Mode voice editing

### Who should use dIKta.me

- Anyone who cares about data privacy and local processing
- Users who want to choose or switch AI models freely
- People tired of paying monthly subscriptions for AI tools
- Developers and power users who want customizable prompts and pipelines
- Bilingual professionals (EN/ES translation mode)
- Users who need vision/screenshot AI analysis

### The cost comparison

Wispr Flow: $144/year. dIKta.me: $20 once. After two months, the math favors dIKta.me. Local mode has zero ongoing costs — your hardware does the work.

## Page 2: Wispr Flow Alternatives (Plural)

**URL (future):** `/alternatives/wispr-flow`
**Meta title:** 5 Best Wispr Flow Alternatives in 2026 (Free & Paid)
**Meta description:** Looking for a Wispr Flow alternative? Compare dIKta.me, Aqua Voice, Dragon, Windows Voice Typing, and Talon — by price, privacy, and features.

### Why people look for Wispr Flow alternatives

Wispr Flow is a strong product, but three things drive people to look elsewhere:

1. **Price.** $12/month adds up. Not everyone wants another AI subscription.
2. **Cloud-only.** Every word goes to Wispr's servers. Privacy-conscious users want local processing.
3. **No model choice.** You use their AI or nothing. No BYOK, no local models, no swapping providers.

### What to look for in an alternative

- **Privacy model:** Does it run locally, or does everything go to the cloud?
- **Pricing:** Subscription vs one-time vs free?
- **Model flexibility:** Can you choose your STT, LLM, and TTS providers?
- **Workflow breadth:** Dictation only, or also refinement, Q&A, vision, translation?
- **Platform:** Windows, Mac, or both?

### The alternatives

#### 1. dIKta.me — Best for privacy and model freedom

- **Price:** $20 one-time (early bird) / Free tier with cloud wallet
- **Platform:** Windows
- **Key strengths:** Local-first (Whisper + Ollama + Kokoro), model-agnostic (4 STT / 5 LLM / 5 TTS), vision AI, 8 workflow modes, MIT open source
- **Limitations:** Windows-only, solo developer, no Command Mode yet
- **Best for:** Privacy-conscious users, power users who want control, people tired of subscriptions

#### 2. Aqua Voice — Best for developers

- **Price:** $8/mo
- **Platform:** Mac, Windows
- **Key strengths:** 97% accuracy on technical terms (kubectl, PyTorch, useEffect), developer vocabulary
- **Limitations:** Cloud-only, tiny team (3 people, $330K ARR), no LLM pipeline, no vision
- **Best for:** Developers who dictate code-heavy content and need technical term accuracy

#### 3. Dragon Professional — Best for legacy enterprise

- **Price:** ~$500 one-time (or $15/mo for Dragon Anywhere)
- **Platform:** Windows (desktop), iOS/Android (Anywhere)
- **Key strengths:** Decades of voice recognition R&D, medical/legal vocabularies, enterprise deployments
- **Limitations:** No AI post-processing, no local LLM, dated UI, cloud-dependent for mobile, expensive
- **Best for:** Enterprises with existing Dragon deployments, medical/legal professionals using specialty vocabularies

#### 4. Windows Voice Typing — Best free basic option

- **Price:** Free (built into Windows 10/11)
- **Platform:** Windows
- **Key strengths:** Zero setup, decent accuracy for casual use, system-level
- **Limitations:** No AI processing, no refinement, no vision, basic punctuation only, no model choice
- **Best for:** Users who need basic dictation and don't want to install anything

#### 5. Talon Voice — Best for accessibility power users

- **Price:** Free (open source)
- **Platform:** Windows, Mac, Linux
- **Key strengths:** Full computer control by voice, eye tracking support, highly customizable scripting
- **Limitations:** Steep learning curve, requires writing custom scripts, focused on computer control rather than text dictation
- **Best for:** Users with motor disabilities who need complete hands-free computer control

## Competitor Data (Centralized Reference)

### Wispr Flow
- **Category:** AI voice dictation
- **Pricing:** $12/mo (annual), $15/mo (monthly)
- **Valuation:** $700M (2025)
- **Platform:** Mac, Windows, iOS, Android
- **Key strength:** Polish, Command Mode, enterprise compliance
- **Key weakness:** Cloud-only, no model choice, subscription
- **Best for:** Enterprise teams, Mac users, managed experience
- **Not ideal for:** Privacy-conscious users, budget-conscious users, power users wanting model control

### Aqua Voice
- **Category:** Developer voice dictation
- **Pricing:** $8/mo
- **Funding:** ~$3M raised
- **Platform:** Mac, Windows
- **Key strength:** 97% accuracy on technical terms
- **Key weakness:** Tiny team (3 people), cloud-only, no AI pipeline beyond transcription
- **Best for:** Developers dictating technical content
- **Not ideal for:** Non-developers, privacy-conscious users, anyone wanting more than dictation

### Dragon NaturallySpeaking
- **Category:** Legacy voice dictation
- **Pricing:** ~$500 one-time (Professional), ~$15/mo (Anywhere)
- **Owner:** Microsoft (via Nuance acquisition)
- **Platform:** Windows (desktop), iOS/Android (Anywhere)
- **Key strength:** Decades of R&D, medical/legal vocabularies, enterprise installed base
- **Key weakness:** Expensive, dated UI, no AI post-processing, slow innovation
- **Best for:** Enterprises with existing deployments, medical/legal specialty vocabularies
- **Not ideal for:** Individual users, anyone wanting modern AI features, budget-conscious users

### Grammarly
- **Category:** AI writing assistant (adjacent competitor)
- **Pricing:** $12/mo (Pro), $15/mo (Business), $30/mo (Enterprise)
- **Valuation:** $13B
- **Platform:** Browser extension, Mac, Windows, iOS, Android
- **Key strength:** 40M daily users, deep grammar/style analysis, wide app coverage
- **Key weakness:** No voice input, no vision, subscription, limited to text refinement
- **Best for:** Writers who type and want grammar/style checking
- **Not ideal for:** Anyone wanting voice input or AI beyond text refinement

### Granola
- **Category:** AI meeting notes (adjacent competitor)
- **Pricing:** $18/mo
- **Valuation:** $1.5B
- **Platform:** Mac, Windows
- **Key strength:** Meeting-specific AI (notes, action items, summaries), no bot (local audio capture)
- **Key weakness:** Meetings only, cloud-only, subscription
- **Best for:** Professionals who need meeting intelligence
- **Not ideal for:** General dictation, non-meeting workflows
