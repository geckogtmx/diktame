# dIKta.me — Brand Book v1.0

---

## 1. The Problem We Solve

Most people think the bottleneck is the AI. It isn't.

**The bottleneck is the input layer.**

You type slower than you think. You correct more than you create. You context-switch between apps to feed your workflows. You pay five separate subscriptions for things that should be one. And you do the glue work — copying, pasting, screenshotting, uploading — that a computer should be doing for you.

dIKta.me removes that bottleneck.

It bridges the three ways you naturally interact with a computer — **your voice, your eyes, and your selected text** — directly to whatever AI you trust, producing results exactly where you need them, without interrupting your flow.

## 2. Mission

> **Work as fast as your mind, not your fingers.**

Or in a single breath:

> *Stop typing at your AI models. Just Talk to them*

## 3. What We Are

dIKta.me is a **local-first AI input interface designed to make Human-AI Interaction (HAI) better** — built for knowledge workers who can't afford to have their thinking slowed down by their tools.

Not just a dictation app. Not just a grammar checker. Not a meeting recorder. Not a clipboard manager. It is all of those things, and the reason you don't need separate subscriptions for any of them.

**The five problems we solve:**

1. **Typing is slow and full of errors.** Speaking is 3–4× faster and requires no backtracking.
2. **Precision input wastes time.** Typos, corrections, re-reads — dIKta.me eliminates the loop.
3. **Workflows require app-switching.** dIKta.me lives at the OS level — it sees what you see, hears what you say, and acts on what you select.
4. **Subscriptions are a tax on productivity.** One app. One price. One time. Everything included.
5. **AI moves fast. Your tools shouldn't lock you in.** dIKta.me is model-agnostic, provider-agnostic, and built to run locally — your data stays yours.

## 5. Audience

### Primary — The Knowledge Worker

**Knowledge workers** — developers, writers, researchers, consultants, analysts, anyone whose job is to produce ideas, documents, and decisions — live inside productivity tools all day. They already use AI (ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini) but are frustrated by the friction between their thoughts and the output. They care about speed, control, and not being locked in.

**They say things like:**
- *"I wish I could just talk to my editor."*
- *"I hate paying for three tools that should be one."*
- *"I don't want my notes going to some cloud I don't control."*

### Secondary — The Privacy-Conscious User

Someone who has avoided cloud AI tools on principle. Local models, self-hosted, open-source. dIKta.me speaks their language: your hardware, your models, your data.

### Tertiary — The Accessibility User

People for whom typing is difficult, painful, or slow. Voice-first isn't a preference — it's a need. dIKta.me treats voice as a first-class input, not an afterthought.

## 6. Personality & Voice

dIKta.me is **precise, direct, and quietly confident**. It doesn't shout. It doesn't over-explain. It respects your time and intelligence.

If dIKta.me were a person, they would be the colleague who solves your problem in two sentences and gets back to work.

### Tone in writing

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Short, declarative sentences | Marketing fluff ("revolutionary", "game-changing") |
| Practical, specific | Vague promises ("takes your productivity to the next level") |
| Honest about limitations | Overclaiming |
| Warm but not cheerful | Emojis in serious copy |
| Respect the user's intelligence | Over-explaining obvious things |

### Voice examples

> ✅ *"Press Ctrl+Alt+S. Point at anything. Get an answer."*
> ❌ *"Experience the revolutionary power of our cutting-edge AI vision technology!"*

> ✅ *"No subscription for the AI. Bring your own key, or run it locally."*
> ❌ *"We believe everyone deserves access to the best AI tools available today."*

## 7. Logo

The dIKta.me logo is a **circle containing the stylised dK monogram** with a flowing lower-right curve representing the `me` — a continuous mark that reads as both initials and motion.

### Variants

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `diktame_SVG_003.svg` | **Primary** — white mark on orange circle. Use on dark backgrounds, app icons, dark web. |
| `diktame_SVG_001.svg` | **Reversed** — orange mark on transparent/white. Use on light backgrounds, print, white web. |
| `diktame_SVG_002.svg` | **Contained** — orange square background, white circle, orange mark. Use when a solid bounding box is required (social avatars, favicons, app stores). |

### Logo rules

- **Minimum size**: 24×24px (icon context) / 48×48px (standalone use)
- **Clear space**: Maintain a margin of at least ¼ of the logo's diameter on all sides
- **Never**: Stretch, rotate, recolour, add drop shadows, or place on a busy background without a solid container
- **Never**: Use the wordmark `dIKta.me` in a different font — it is always set in **Inter**

## 9. Typography

### Primary Typeface — Inter

All dIKta.me UI, documentation, and marketing uses **Inter** (open-source, Google Fonts).

| Role | Weight | Size guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Display / Hero | ExtraBold 800 | 48–72px |
| Heading H1 | Bold 700 | 32–40px |
| Heading H2 | SemiBold 600 | 24–28px |
| Body | Regular 400 | 15–16px |
| Body emphasis | Medium 500 | 15–16px |
| Caption / Label | Regular 400 | 12–13px |
| Code / Hotkeys | Monospace (system) | match body |

### Type rules

- Headlines are **sentence case**, not Title Case (matches app and docs tone)
- Hotkeys are always formatted in code style: `Ctrl + Alt + D`
- The product name is always written **dIKta.me** — lowercase `d`, uppercase `IK`, lowercase `ta.me`. Never `Diktame`, `DIKTAME`, or `diktame`.

## 10. Name & Wordmark

The product name is **dIKta.me**.

The capitalisation is intentional and meaningful:
- **dIK** — dictate
- **ta.me** — the `.me` TLD embedded in the name, making the domain itself the brand

In running text, always write `dIKta.me`. In URLs, `dIKta.me`. In code or filenames, `diktame`.

The tagline that pairs with the wordmark:

> *Stop typing at your AI models. Just talk to them.*

---

## 11. Philosophy

dIKta.me is built on four principles that inform every product and marketing decision:

1. **Local-first.** Your data lives on your machine. Cloud is an option, never a requirement.
2. **Model-agnostic.** We don't bet on one AI provider. You choose — or switch — freely.
3. **Open.** Prompts are exposed. Configuration is yours. The roadmap is public.
4. **Convergent.** One app, every input method, any workflow. We replace subscriptions, not add to them.

---

## 12. What We Are Not

- Not a "dictation app" — that undersells it
- Not an "AI assistant" — we don't answer questions, we route your inputs to the AI that does
- Not cloud-dependent — local mode is a first-class citizen, not a fallback
- Not locked in — no proprietary model, no forced subscription, no walled garden
