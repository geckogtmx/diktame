## Part 3: Daily Content Engine (April 16+)

### The System: Blog -> Social in 15 min/day

You already write daily AI/tech news on LinkedIn + Substack (bilingual). Each blog post feeds FB/IG:

**Daily repurposing flow:**
1. Publish blog post (LinkedIn + Substack) -- already happening
2. Extract the sharpest hook + 1-2 key points (5 min)
3. Create one IG format: carousel, quote card, or Reel voiceover (5 min in Canva/CapCut)
4. Write FB version: hook + 2-3 paragraph opinion + CTA (5 min)
5. Schedule both via Meta Business Suite

### News-to-dIKta.me Bridge

Every AI/tech story is a chance to reinforce positioning:

| News topic | dIKta.me angle |
|------------|----------------|
| AI model releases | "New model? dIKta.me already supports it. Model-agnostic." |
| Subscription price hikes | "Another AI tool raised prices. dIKta.me: $20. Once." |
| Privacy/data scandals | "Your voice on their servers, or on your hardware. Your call." |
| Local AI advances | "Runs on your laptop. dIKta.me had it built in from day one." |
| Voice AI news | "Voice is the future input layer. We've been saying this." |
| Productivity tool launches | "Another subscription. Or one app that does it all." |

### Weekly Content Mix

| Day | Instagram | Facebook | Source |
|-----|-----------|----------|--------|
| **Mon** | Reel: workflow demo | Same video + longer caption | Original |
| **Tue** | Carousel: news hot take | Text + image: news commentary | Blog repurpose |
| **Wed** | Story: dev update / poll | Link post: Substack article | Blog repurpose |
| **Thu** | Reel: feature spotlight | Text + image: pain point hook | Original |
| **Fri** | Carousel: educational | Text post: build-in-public | Original |
| **Sat** | Story: casual / news reaction | -- | Light repurpose |
| **Sun** | -- (or carousel if great story) | -- | Rest |

**Total**: ~5 IG feed posts + daily stories, ~4 FB posts/week
**Repurposed from blog**: ~40% | **Original**: ~60%

### Bilingual Strategy

- **Feed posts**: Primarily EN (wider reach). ES versions as separate posts 1-2x/week -- pick the stories that resonate more with Spanish-speaking audience.
- **Stories**: Alternate EN/ES. Quick text overlays are easy to redo in Spanish.
- **Reels**: EN voiceover/captions. Add ES caption track via CapCut or IG's auto-captions.
- **FB**: EN primary. Consider a pinned comment in ES on key posts, or alternate-day ES posts if engagement warrants it.

## Part 5: Visual Style (Quick Reference)

- **Background**: #0A0918 (Midnight) -- matches app, premium on feeds
- **Accent**: #C55A28 (Brand Orange) -- highlights, CTAs
- **Font**: Inter, sentence case, white on dark
- **Screen recordings**: Midnight theme, clean desktop, no personal files visible
- **No stock photos**. Real screenshots, real recordings, real terminal output.
- **Aspect ratios**: 1:1 feed, 9:16 Reels/Stories, 4:5 carousels
- **Logo**: SVG_002 (contained) for profile pics, SVG_003 (white on orange) for watermarks
