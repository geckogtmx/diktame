# dIKta.me — HAI Post
**Human-AI Interaction angle**

---

## LinkedIn 🇺🇸

> **Everyone's racing to make AI smarter. Nobody's fixing how you talk to it.**
>
> We've gone from GPT-3 to reasoning models in 3 years. Context windows went from 4K to 1M tokens. Multimodal, real-time, on-device — the intelligence side of the equation is moving fast.
>
> But here's what hasn't changed: you still type at it like it's a search engine from 2003.
>
> You open a browser tab. You paste text. You upload a screenshot manually. You switch apps to copy context. You pay five different subscriptions for tools that should be one.
>
> The bottleneck was never the AI. **The bottleneck is the input layer.**
>
> Human-AI Interaction (HAI) is the next frontier — and it's embarrassingly underbuilt. The models are ready. The interface isn't.
>
> dIKta.me is built to close that gap for knowledge workers:
> - 🎙️ **Voice** → text, formatted, injected at your cursor. 3-4× faster than typing.
> - 👁️ **Vision** → point at anything on your screen, ask a question, get an answer. No copy-paste.
> - ✂️ **Selection** → highlight text, refine/translate/ask about it. One hotkey.
> - 🏠 **Local-first** → your models, your hardware, your data. No cloud required.
>
> One app. Every input method. Any model. No new subscriptions.
>
> Better HAI starts here.
>
> 🔗 dikta.me
