# Spinach.ai Review (vs dIKta.me SPEC_001)

## Overview
[Spinach.ai](https://www.spinach.ai) is positioned very specifically as an **"AI Scrum Master"** and agile meeting assistant. While Granola is a "smart notepad" and Fellow is "meeting lifecycle management", Spinach targets product and engineering teams by focusing heavily on **issue tracker integrations** and sprint automation.

## Key Features & Differentiators
1. **Deep Ticket Context & Tagging:** This is their "killer feature." Spinach listens to the meeting, identifies when a specific Jira, Linear, or Trello ticket is mentioned, and automatically **tags that ticket** in the summary. 
2. **One-Click Ticket Creation:** It proactively recommends new issue tracker tickets based on the conversation, allowing the user to create them with a single click.
3. **Targeted Slack Routing:** Instead of just dumping a summary in a channel, Spinach routes specific meeting summaries to specific channels, and sends **personal to-do lists via direct message** to the exact person assigned an action item.
4. **Platform Omnipresence:** It automatically joins/processes Zoom, Google Meet, MS Teams, Webex, and *Slack Huddles*.

## What's New/Good to Note for dIKta.me?

* **Entity Extraction for Tickets:** If dIKta.me ever implements the "Task tool sync" (currently marked Phase 4), doing what Spinach does—mapping spoken references like "Bug one-oh-five" to an actual Jira/Linear URL—is a huge value-add for dev teams. 
* **Targeted Action Item Delivery:** While dIKta.me is local-first, if you build the Slack/Teams integrations, sending direct messages for action items rather than just saving a markdown file is a highly effective workflow pattern.
* **Persona Positioning:** "Scribe" is a generalist tool. Spinach proves that choosing a hyper-specific persona (e.g., "AI Scrum Master") simplifies the marketing and guides the feature set. dIKta.me's "Local-First/Privacy-First Scribe" is a similarly strong, distinct angle.

## Alignment with SPEC_001
* Spinach strongly validates the **Phase 4+ integrations** (Linear, Jira, Slack). Customers clearly want meeting insights pushed directly into their system of record.
* Spinach does not seem to offer the "User's Typed Notes as Intent Signals" feature that dIKta.me is building. Like Fellow, it relies primarily on passively transcribing the whole meeting and using AI to pick out the important parts. dIKta.me's approach of using the user's active notes to guide the summary remains a strong, unique differentiator.
* Because Spinach relies heavily on cloud integrations to do its magic, dIKta.me's **local-first system audio capture** remains a clear advantage for privacy-conscious users who don't want an AI reading their CRMs or tracking their Slack DMs.
